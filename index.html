Mohamed Elsayed

Dept. of Computing Science, University of Alberta

I am a PhD student at the RLAI lab, working with Rupam Mahmood and affiliated with Amii. I develop scalable algorithms for continual learning from streaming data, addressing fundamental challenges in real-world AI systems.

My research spans multiple critical areas: (1) streaming deep reinforcement learning, developing both model-free and model-based methods for real-time learning; (2) continual learning, allowing learners to adapt indefinitely without performance degradation; and (3) scalable recurrent architectures for efficient sequence modeling. My work has been published at leading ML conferences (NeurIPS, ICML, ICLR), with implementations deployed on real robotic systems.

Prior to this, I completed my master's degree from the University of Alberta and my bachelor's degree in Communication and Information Engineering from Zewail City of Science and Technology, Egypt.

Outside of research: I'm passionate about photography. I also enjoy observing animal behavior and intelligence, particularly ravens.
